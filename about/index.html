About CrawlMedia

A creative team built for working across borders.

CrawlMedia is an outsourced creative studio in Kathmandu, Nepal. We help brands, agencies and creators around the world with video editing, branding and SEO, delivered remotely with a clear scope from day one.

Start a project Explore services

01 Why we exist

Great creative work shouldn’t depend on your postcode.

Growing teams need a steady flow of edited video, consistent brand assets and a website that people can find. Hiring specialists for each of those in-house is slow and expensive, and juggling freelancers takes time most teams don’t have.

We started CrawlMedia to offer a simpler option: one remote partner with three focused specialties, transparent pricing in USD, and a process designed for teams in different time zones.

Every engagement starts with listening. We learn your audience, goals and references, agree on exactly what we will deliver, and then get to work while your team focuses on everything else.

02 What we believe

How we like to work.

Four principles that shape every brief, every edit and every report we send.

01

Clarity first

Scope, deliverables, revisions and timelines are agreed in writing before any work begins, so everyone knows what “done” looks like.

02

Craft over volume

We would rather deliver fewer pieces that work hard for your brand than flood your channels with forgettable content.

03

Honest expectations

We tell you what a plan includes, what it doesn’t, and what results depend on. No guaranteed rankings, no vague promises.

04

Remote by design

Our process is built for working across time zones: clear briefs, milestone check-ins and consolidated feedback rounds.

Working with teams in North America, Europe, Australia and Asia, our hours often overlap with your evening or morning, so work moves forward while you are offline.
