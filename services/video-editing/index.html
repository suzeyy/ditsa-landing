Service terms & what to expect +
  • All services are delivered digitally. No physical printing, production, filming, or shipping is included unless separately agreed.
  • Project timelines begin after payment and receipt of all required content, access, and brand materials.
  • A revision round means one consolidated set of feedback from the client. Changes outside the approved brief may be billed separately.
  • Any work beyond the listed package scope will be quoted and approved before it begins.
  • For monthly services, payment is due at the start of each billing cycle. Unused monthly deliverables do not roll over unless agreed in writing.
  • Video editing clients must provide organized raw footage, scripts, and required brand assets. The video packages assume a maximum of 5 minutes of supplied raw footage per short-form video.
  • Video packages exclude filming, scripting, voiceover recording, content strategy, complex VFX, and advanced compositing unless separately quoted.
